Every one of us leans on a natural behaviour style — the way we react when a deadline moves, when someone disagrees, or when we walk into a room full of strangers. This test puts a name to yours.

What you'll get at the end

  • Your dominant bird type, with a full percentage breakdown across all four
  • Your secondary "wing" — the type that colours how your main style lands on others
  • Honest strengths and blind spots, plus how you behave under stress
  • Where you thrive at work, in relationships, and how others should approach you
Answer honestly, not aspirationally. Pick the option that sounds most like you on an ordinary day, not the one you wish were true — your first instinct is almost always the accurate one. The four bird types are deliberately kept hidden until the end so that knowing them can't sway your answers.
